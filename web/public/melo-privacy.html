Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Melo Korean Fusion Cuisine ("we", "us", "Melo") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you interact with us through our Facebook Messenger chatbot and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Messenger interactions: When you send a message to our Facebook Page, we receive your Facebook sender ID and the content of your messages.
  • Usage data: We may log request timestamps and message types for debugging and service improvement purposes.

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or sensitive personal data through our chatbot.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries about our menu, opening hours, and restaurant information.
  • To improve the quality and accuracy of our automated responses.
  • To diagnose technical issues with our messaging service.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Your message text is processed by the Claude AI API to generate responses. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • Meta (Facebook): Messages are transmitted via the Facebook Messenger Platform under Meta's data policies.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or government authority.

4. Data Retention

We do not permanently store the content of your Messenger conversations. System logs (excluding message content) may be retained for up to 30 days for debugging purposes.

5. Your Rights

  • You may stop interacting with our chatbot at any time.
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us directly.
  • You can manage your Facebook data through your Facebook account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
